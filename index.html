✨ What Is Desktop-Gamepad?

Desktop-Gamepad is a clever little tool that lets you use an Xbox controller as a complete mouse and keyboard on your Windows computer. It's designed for anyone who wants to control their PC from the comfort of their couch, just like using a console.

You plug in your controller, launch the app, and your left stick moves the cursor while your right stick acts as a scroll wheel. Click a text box and an on-screen keyboard automatically pops up for typing. It's that simple.

🚀 Getting Started

Follow these three easy steps and you'll be up and running in under five minutes.

  1. Download the software. Visit this link to download the application: github.com/mamen9000/Desktop-Gamepad
  2. Run the file. Once downloaded, double-click the downloaded file to open it. Your computer might ask you to confirm you want to run it — just click "Yes" or "Run".
  3. Connect your controller. Plug in your Xbox controller via USB or connect it wirelessly with Bluetooth. Open Desktop-Gamepad and start moving your cursor with the left stick.

🛠️ Installation & Setup

System Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • An Xbox controller (Wireless via Bluetooth or wired via USB)
  • Some older controllers require a USB cable — but most modern ones work wirelessly
  • No administrator rights or special drivers are needed

💡 Tip: If you're using a wireless controller, make sure it's paired with your PC before opening Desktop-Gamepad. Bluetooth pairs easily in Windows Settings.

Your First Launch

  1. Open the app. Double-click the Desktop-Gamepad icon on your desktop or in your Downloads folder.
  2. Connect your controller. If your controller isn't already on, turn it on now. A vibration or light indicates it's connected.
  3. Start moving. Gently push the left stick — you should see your mouse cursor glide across the screen. Push the right stick up and down to scroll.

🔥 Pro Tips

  • Set a comfortable cursor speed. Too fast and it feels twitchy; too slow and it feels sluggish. Adjust it in the settings dialog until it feels natural to you.
  • Press the A button to click. It's the most natural and comfortable button for clicking. The left trigger also right-clicks by default.
  • Use the B button to go back. In many apps, pressing B behaves like pressing the Backspace or Escape key, which is handy for navigation.
  • Try different dead zones. If the cursor drifts slightly when you're not touching the stick, tweak the dead zone setting for a smoother experience.

🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to use?

Yes! Desktop-Gamepad is completely free and open source. You can download it, use it, and even view its code if you're curious.

Does it work with PS4 or Switch controllers?

Currently, it's optimized for Xbox controllers. Xbox controllers are the most compatible with Windows and offer the smoothest experience. You might be able to connect others via third-party software, but we recommend an Xbox controller for the best results.

Will it interfere with my normal mouse and keyboard?

Not at all. You can switch between your mouse and the controller whenever you want. They work simultaneously without any conflicts. Use whichever feels more natural at any moment.

Is it safe for my computer?

Absolutely. Desktop-Gamepad is a light, clean application that doesn't alter system files or require installation in any invasive way. Just download, run, and enjoy.
